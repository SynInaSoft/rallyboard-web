Two things most rotation sheets don't have

Points won and lost in each rotation

Most rotation sheets only show where players stand. The rotation tracker has a plus box and a minus box under every rotation. After the set you can see which rotation scores and which one needs work, instead of guessing from the final score.

How to use the rotation tracker

  1. Before the set, write your starting six in the Rotation 1 court by jersey number. Zone 1 is the first server.
  2. For every rally, make a mark in the plus or minus box of the rotation you were in when the rally started.
  3. When you win a rally on the other team's serve, you rotate. Move to the next rotation box.
  4. After the set, write your best rotation and the rotation to fix at the bottom.

Questions

Are these really free?

Yes. Download them, print them and copy them for your team. There is no email or sign-up. Please don't sell them.

Will they print on A4 paper?

The sheets are US Letter. On A4 paper, choose “Fit to page” or “Scale to fit” in the print dialog.

Which rotation is rotation 1?

Coaches number rotations differently. On these sheets, rotation 1 has the setter (S in the 5-1, S1 in the 6-2 and 4-2) in zone 1, and each next rotation is one clockwise turn later.

Do the charts show serve-receive formations?

No. They show base positions by zone, because receive formations depend on your team. Each chart has a blank serve-receive plan section. To draw receive formations and check them for overlap, see the overlap rule and RallyBoard.
