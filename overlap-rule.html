Volleyball overlap rule (7.4)What changed in the FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028

Updated 26 October 2026

Short answer: since the FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028, only the receiving team must be in rotational order when the server hits the ball. The players of the serving team are free to stand anywhere on their own court. An overlap (positional fault) can therefore only be called on the receiving team, unless your competition still uses the older wording.

The rule in the 2025–2028 text

At the moment the ball is hit by the server, each team must be positioned within its own court (except the server). The players of the receiving team must be in the rotational order at the service hit. The players of the serving team, however, are free to occupy any position at the service hit.FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028, rule 7.4

Positions are numbered 4, 3, 2 in the front row (left to right, facing the net) and 5, 6, 1 in the back row. Each player is compared only with the players next to them in that order.

The check uses the feet that touch the floor (rule 7.4.3). A back-row player must have at least part of one foot further from the centre line than the front foot of the matching front-row player. A player on the right (or left) must have at least part of one foot closer to that sideline than the other player in the same row. Being level counts as legal.

After the service hit, players of both teams may move anywhere (rule 7.4.4). After a positional fault the team loses the rally (the opponent wins the point and the service), and the positions are corrected (rule 7.5.4).

Many national federations, school and youth competitions adopted the new rule in 2025, but some leagues still require the serving team to be in order. Check the rulebook your competition uses.

The seven pairs to check

In a receive formation, only these adjacent pairs can create an overlap:

  • Front and back (same column): 4 in front of 5, 3 in front of 6, 2 in front of 1.
  • Left and right (same row): 4 left of 3, 3 left of 2 in the front row; 5 left of 6, 6 left of 1 in the back row.

Players who are not next to each other are never compared. For example, position 4 may stand behind position 1, and the setter in position 1 may stand close to the net as long as they stay to the right of 6 and behind 2.

Questions

Can the serving team commit an overlap in 2025?

Not under the FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028. Rule 7.4 says the players of the serving team are free to occupy any position at the service hit. They must still be within their own court, and the server must serve from the service zone.

Which team can be called for a positional fault?

The receiving team. Its players must be in rotational order at the moment of the service hit, compared only with the adjacent players in their row and column.

Does the libero count in the overlap check?

Yes. The libero takes the position of the back-row player they replaced, usually a middle blocker in position 5, 6 or 1, and is compared like any other player in that position.
